Self-drill vs drilling others

Your own plot: you get the stones + $DRILL find + planter bonus + the owner bonus — you keep everything.
Someone else's open plot: you get stones + find + planter bonus, but the owner bonus goes to them.
Others on your open plot: they take the drilling risk, and you collect the owner bonus for free.
Rule: always drill your own plots (full capture), and keep them open so visitors also pay you. Only drill others if their Power Station speed beats yours — none exist yet, so self-drill wins outright.

Ore / stones held · value · use

Value = in-game $DRILL rating. Burn stones to upgrade plot levels (rarer stones for higher levels) or sell them on the market. Slush is a byproduct with no use yet.

Resource flow

Rig (costs $DRILL, or free credit) → produces a tray:
 • Stones → burn to upgrade level → more weight → bigger ETH-deposit share & owner bonus; or sell for DRILL.
 • $DRILL find → buy rigs / buildings / trade; sell for ETH.
 • Owner bonus ($DRILL) → to the plot's owner.
Level unlocks buildings: Power Station (rig speed, draws drillers), Ore Processor (bigger owner bonus), Satellite (more weight).
ETH flows from the DRILL/ETH pool into the treasury, split to plots by weight.

Attract drillers — hub strategy turn plots into toll booths

A visiting driller keeps their full find on any plot, so the only thing that pulls them to yours is a Power Station (speeds their rig +5/10/20%, visitors included). "Attracting people" = build Power Stations on open plots, then collect the owner bonus every time someone drills them.
The loop: upgrade a plot to L10 (unlocks buildings) → build Power Station (draws drillers) + Ore Processor (bigger owner bonus per visit) + Satellite (more weight) → keep it open → passive $DRILL from every visitor, plus a bigger slice of ETH deposits.
Buildings unlock at level 10 — a heavy stone spend (top rungs need rare stones incl. Core Blue), so hubs are a mid-game play. Until you can build a Power Station, "open" only catches random traffic; it doesn't actively attract. Concentrate on a few hubs, not all 60 plots.
